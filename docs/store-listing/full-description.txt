KoLmafia Mobile is an unofficial fan client for the free browser game Kingdom of Loathing.

• Log in over HTTPS to play on your phone or tablet
• Adventure, inventory, skills, familiars, chat, shop, and mall
• Run ASH scripts with an on-device editor and console
• Relay browser for KoL pages inside the app
• Open source under the GNU General Public License
